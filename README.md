# NOA Dental Clinic – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

NOA Dental Clinic is an established multi-specialty dental healthcare provider in Dubai, operating since 2005 with branches in Trade Centre and Jumeirah Lake Towers (JLT). The clinic offers preventive, restorative, cosmetic, orthodontic and implant dentistry, with an emphasis on ethical treatment, natural tooth preservation and personalised patient care.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating NOA's clinical expertise, improving treatment discovery and creating a more credible, patient-focused experience.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. More Authentic Hero Presentation
- Replaced the abstract SVG smile illustration with a stronger focus on authentic clinic photography.
- Prioritised real clinical environments, dental professionals and patient-care imagery.
- Improved the opportunity to communicate the clinic's identity through genuine visual content.

**Problem addressed:** The previous hero relied on abstract artwork rather than real clinic photography, making the first impression less personal and potentially weakening credibility.

### 2. More Detailed Treatment Information
- Improved the organisation of the six main treatment categories.
- Strengthened treatment descriptions to communicate individual services and their relevance to patients.
- Increased the visibility of specialist expertise associated with relevant treatments.

**Problem addressed:** The previous treatment sections relied on generic illustrations and broad descriptions that did not sufficiently explain individual treatments, benefits or specialist involvement.

### 3. Stronger Trust and Appointment Journey
- Reviewed the presentation of credibility information, including the 50,000+ patients-served claim.
- Avoided presenting unverified opening hours as confirmed information.
- Improved appointment enquiry pathways while distinguishing WhatsApp enquiries from a connected booking system.

**Problem addressed:** The previous homepage included credibility information and branch opening-hour details requiring verification, while its WhatsApp-based enquiry journey lacked a dedicated booking form or connected appointment system.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Prioritised authentic clinic imagery over abstract illustrations to create a more personal dental healthcare experience.
- **Layout:** Improved the organisation of treatment categories, specialist information and clinic details.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Strengthened the presentation of treatment descriptions and clinical expertise.
- **User Experience:** Made treatment exploration and appointment enquiry pathways clearer.
- **Consistency:** Improved the presentation of both clinic locations, treatment information and credibility elements.
- **Trust:** Distinguished verified business information from claims and details requiring confirmation.

## Project Objective

The redesigned homepage aims to strengthen NOA Dental Clinic's professional credibility, communicate its ethical and multi-specialty approach to dentistry, improve treatment discovery and provide clearer pathways for patient enquiries across its two Dubai locations.
